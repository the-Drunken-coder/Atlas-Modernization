# Movement history preserves reported values

Status: accepted from design interview rounds 1 through 3 on 2026-09-09. The user confirmed the final shared understanding on 2026-09-09; runtime implementation remains pending.

1. **Time & Date:** 2026-09-09, America/New_York.
2. **Name:** Preserve independent movement readings without inventing fresh observations.
3. **Context:** Low-bandwidth sources can omit unchanged fields. A stationary Sentry may report a battery change without repeating its position. Requiring every movement report to contain coordinates loses independent speed and altitude readings, while copying the current Entity makes omitted values look newly observed.
4. **Decision:** Retain independently supplied position, speed and altitude with their observation time when known and Atlas arrival time. Omitted movement quantities remain absent in the sample; unrelated changes add nothing to movement history. Retain samples for 30 days from observation time when known, otherwise arrival time. Backfill changes historical data only, including when an Entity has no current location. Keep reports with unknown measurement time using explicitly labeled arrival time. Do not provide manual correction or deletion of historical samples in v1.
5. **Alternatives considered:** Coordinate-required samples discard requested speed/altitude history and unnecessarily couple bandwidth use to history capture. Saving merged state creates false freshness. Starting retention at upload time for dated reports preserves arbitrarily old measurements despite a 30-day observation window. Allowing backfill to update current state adds reconciliation behavior outside this feature. Manual historical editing adds an operator workflow not required for the first release.
6. **Consequences:** Raw history and map geometry are different views: a movement sample can have no position. Historical inspection may show an earlier known reading, while preserving its original report time. Avoid redundant field timestamps; the display shows an age cue when a value was reported more than one minute before the inspected historical time, with exact times still inspectable. A report measured 29 days ago and received today has one day remaining. Mixed-age uploads keep eligible reports and skip expired reports with clear counts. Unknown measurement time remains explicit rather than fabricated. Automatic expiry is separate from manual historical editing.
